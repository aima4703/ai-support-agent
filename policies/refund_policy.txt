Refund Policy

Refunds are only issued for items returned in accordance with our Return Policy.

If an order arrives damaged or defective, customers are entitled to a full refund or free replacement, regardless of the 30-day window, as long as it is reported within 7 days of delivery.

We do not offer partial refunds for change-of-mind returns; the full item price is refunded once the returned item is received and inspected.

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item sent, damaged in transit, etc.).
