Return Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund, as long as the item is unused and in its original packaging.

Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

To start a return, contact support with your order number. We will email you a prepaid return shipping label.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
